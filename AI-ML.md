#### Machine Learning
Learns in 3 general ways:
- **Supervised Learning**: Learning from labeled data
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Find hidden patterns in unlabeled data
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Learning by trial and error. Gets rewards or penalties.
