- Deep Learning: Modern way to teach AI

### Machine Learning
Learns in 3 general ways:
- **Supervised Learning**: Learning from labeled data
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Find hidden patterns in unlabeled data
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Learning by trial and error. Gets rewards or penalties.

#### Supervised Learning
2 types of SL are:
- **Regression**: Predicts a number.
- **Classification:** Puts things into categories.

Algorithms for SL:
1. **Linear Regression** (Regression)
2. **Logistic Regression** (Classification)
3. **Decision Tree** (Regression & Classification (CART))
