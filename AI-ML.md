## Machine Learning
Learns in 3 general ways:
- **Supervised Learning**: Learning from labeled data
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Find hidden patterns in unlabeled data
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Learning by trial and error. Gets rewards or penalties.

#### Supervised Learning
2 types of SL are:
- **Regression**: Predicts a number.
- **Classification:** Puts things into categories.

Algorithms for SL:
1. **Linear Regression** (Regression): Finding a straight line to predict exact numbers.
2. **Logistic Regression** (Classification): Predicting the chance/ percentage of something.
3. **Decision Tree** (Regression & Classification (CART)): Asking a series of yes/ no questions to make a decision.
